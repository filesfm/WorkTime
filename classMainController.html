Detailed Description

QML-facing controller behind Main.qml.

Instantiated directly from QML (not a singleton). Owns whether time tracking is currently active, and acts as the sole QML-facing facade over Settings — Settings itself stays plain C++ and is never exposed to QML directly; MainController's setters forward to it, and its property change signals are relayed from Settings' own.
